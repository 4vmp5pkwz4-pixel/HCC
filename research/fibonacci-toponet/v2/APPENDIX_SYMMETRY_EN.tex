\section{Exact reversal obstruction and group-averaged representations}
\label{sec:fibonacci_symmetry_obstruction}
Let $R$ reverse a length-$T$ token sequence. Consider any stack of shared-token
linear maps, tokenwise nonlinearities, layer normalization, and bidirectional
self-attention with masks depending only on $|i-j|$. Then each layer $F$
is $R$-equivariant: $F(Rx)=RF(x)$. A permutation-invariant pooling map $H$
obeys $H(Rz)=H(z)$. Consequently every downstream classifier $f=h\circ H\circ F$
is reversal invariant,
\begin{equation}
 f(Rx)=f(x).
\end{equation}
For a paired classification distribution in which $(x,y)$ and $(Rx,1-y)$
have equal masses, the accuracy of every deterministic reversal-invariant
classifier is exactly $1/2$. Its minimal pair-averaged binary cross entropy
is $\log 2$, because $-\tfrac12\log p-\tfrac12\log(1-p)\ge\log2$.

For a finite group $G$ of input transformations, group averaging defines
\begin{equation}
 (\mathcal P_G f)(x)=\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}f(gx),\quad
 \mathcal P_G^2=\mathcal P_G,\quad
 (\mathcal P_G f)(gx)=(\mathcal P_G f)(x).
\end{equation}
For a unitary representation $U$ on a Hilbert space the corresponding
operator $|G|^{-1}\sum_g U(g)$ is an orthogonal projector onto invariant vectors.
This is the same representation-theoretic mechanism as deck-group projection
on $S^3/\Gamma$, but does not make learned representations physical cosmological
observables.

A stable Fibonacci-inspired two-state block is
\begin{equation}
 C_\phi=\frac{1}{\phi}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \operatorname{spec}(C_\phi)=\{1,-\phi^{-2}\},\quad\|C_\phi\|_2=1.
\end{equation}
For $d$ even, let $C=I_{d/2}\otimes C_\phi$ and
$W=0.8 C+0.1\tanh(\Delta)/d$ (elementwise $\tanh$). Then
$\|W\|_2\le0.8+0.1\|\tanh(\Delta)/d\|_F\le0.9$.
This is a sufficient contraction certificate, not a physical derivation
of a Fibonacci law of intelligence.
