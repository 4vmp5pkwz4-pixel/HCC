## 2. Exact coordinate isomorphism
For positive M,R define m=M/mP, r=R/lP, C=2m/r, Q=1/(mr), u=log10(m), v=log10(r), a=log10(C), b=log10(Q), k=log10(2). Then (a,b)=(k+u-v,-u-v); inverse u=(a-b-k)/2, v=(k-a-b)/2. Jacobian determinant -2, globally bijective on positive quadrant after logs. C Q=2/r², C/Q=2m². C=Q=1 gives m=1/sqrt(2), r=sqrt(2), not (1,1). Constant density slope 1/3 in mass-radius plot. In (a,b) plot constant R slope -1, constant M slope +1; constant Euclidean equivalent density a+b/2=constant (because u-3v=2a+b-2k). These are algebra, not dynamical laws. C,Q are proxies for typed values, not classifiers of all physical regimes: atomic binding, many-body coherence, charge, spin, temperature, interactions and geometry are independent. A large C is not universally a black hole, Q>=1 not a universal forbidden zone. Photon E/c² frame-dependent, wavelength/2pi not size, no rest-mass point. Density is meaningful as Euclidean-equivalent only when M/R semantics permit it. Information bits are entropy proxy at an applicable horizon, not physical objects.

## 3. FBS3R

## 4. Conditional S³ / GR
Spatial metric on a fixed round slice dl²=A²(dchi²+sin²chi dOmega²). D=A chi (geodesic), R_area=A sin chi, surface=4pi R_area², V=2pi A³(chi-sin(2chi)/2), V_total=2pi² A³, fraction=(chi-sin(2chi)/2)/pi. Different chi and pi-chi share area, complement volumes. D and V increase monotonically while area shrinks beyond equator. At antipode R_area=0, V=V_total; no logarithmic point; never interpret as infinite density. Flat limit chi->0, V~4pi D³/3. For uniform mass-equivalent total density rho, proper integral rho V differs from M_MS=4pi rho R_area³/3 in homogeneous FLRW with effective Lambda energy included. Not a globally conserved total mass in closed universe. FLRW H>0, Omega_K=-k_curvature c²/(A²H²), with k_curvature=+1 for closed; A=c/(H sqrt(-Omega_K)). R_H=c/H; R_A=c/sqrt(H²+c²/A²)=R_H/sqrt(1-Omega_K); rho=3H²(1-Omega_K)/(8pi G), 2GM_MS(R_A)/(c² R_A)=1. At R_H with total density the compactness is 1-Omega_K when this areal radius is realizable. R_H may exceed A in strongly closed examples; don't show a sphere at impossible areal radius. Near and far chi roots exist; apparent horizon is not the particle/event horizon and not an isolated Schwarzschild black hole. HCC published S³ preset must be kept separate from consistency-derived FLRW preset; no silent fit of carrier radius or horizon. Cosmology observations do not establish S³ topology. At H0=67.4 and OmegaK=-0.001 benchmark: see benchmarks.txt. S³ numerical kernel implements geometry/FLRW relationships, not time evolution or optical distance; expanding universe screen conversion requires model-specific light cone integration and epoch.
